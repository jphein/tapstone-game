# 0009 — Touch on, with a card-only path for every prompt
Date: 2026-09-20 · Brainstorm ruling (Q10)

The shrine's touchscreen is used for the prompts a tap cannot express: cast-or-charge, lane choice,
targets, pass. **Every prompt is also completable with cards alone**: a 5-second countdown resolves
to the rules' default (cast; the lane with the fewest of your units; the nearest legal target), a
second tap of the same card within 3 s means charge, and tapping your castle passes. Acceptance
test for every screen: the game is playable with the glass covered.
