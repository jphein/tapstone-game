# 0039: The Tea House and its Dueling Grounds, where every digital table sits
Date: 2026-09-26 · JP's ruling (on the Quest 2, mid-spike): "the beautiful fantasy world … it's a teahouse at the nexus of worlds and doors appear to different planes"

**The source is JP's LitRPG novel *Inner Authority* and its world bible, the Luna Multiverse**
(Notion: https://app.notion.com/p/Inner-Authority-3064247a8a4080f7a7a6f0d80b9ef2e5). There the **Tea House** is
"the central hub, sanctuary and gateway to all unlocked realms" (summarised in
os.realm.watch's design spec, 2026-03-27). **Tapstone uses that canon; it doesn't invent a parallel one.**
Names, planes, the teahouse's keepers and any door's look come from the bible. Where the bible is silent, a
proposal goes to JP; it never lands as fact.

## The rule (amended 2026-09-26, after reading the bible)

The bible (Part II, "The Tea House" and "The Deck System"; World-building Quick Reference v1.0) is more
exact than the first draft of this rule, so the rule follows it:

- **The Tea House is where you gather; the match is a duel on the Dueling Grounds.** The canon Tea House is
  a *safe zone*: a duel starts there, and **the red door**
  carries the players' decks off to a battlefield in a pocket realm (Book 1, Ch 22). So every digital
  table has two places:
  - **The Tea House:** lantern light, magical tea, comfortable seating and a Shinto/Mayan design influence.
    The lobby is here: seating, joining, the rematch, the result beat and the ledger's chest. Its
    intelligence "offers, never forces", which makes it the natural voice for 0032's one-sentence hints.
  - **The Dueling Grounds:** the red door opens, and the board unrolls in a pocket dimension. The board,
    its rules and the arena are unchanged (0028, 0037).
- **The doors to the realms** stand around the Tea House as scenery. Their canon destinations are the
  Hearthlands, **the Deep Tides** (the blue door, Book 2), the Forge Peaks, the Wandering Courts, the Star
  Fields and the Dreaming. A door reacts to play (a glow or a breath of its weather), and never carries
  rules or offers a choice the table doesn't.
  - **Ruled by JP, 2026-09-26:** Tide's cards come from **the Deep Tides** and Ember's from **the Forge Peaks**.
    Card art, door colours and door weather follow from this.
  - **Ruled by JP, 2026-09-27:** the neutral cards come from **the Hearthlands**. Their card art and print
    frames follow from this; a door's look stays a PROPOSAL wherever the bible is silent.
  - **Ruled by JP, same night:** the lead fills the remaining `LORE:` slots (the keeper, the door looks, the
    Dueling Grounds) from the *Inner Authority* bible. Anything the bible doesn't state is marked PROPOSAL for
    JP, never landed as canon.
- **Cards follow the bible's aesthetics:** "3D and holographic … the art moves … when you play a card it
  animates fully into the world". That is the brief for the headset's card and summon animations.
  - A draw is "the order in which your Deck's elements arrive via teleportation", so a draw tap shows the
    card arriving, not being dealt.
- **In mixed reality, the player's own room is the Tea House.** The red door opens as a portal beside the
  real table, and the Dueling Grounds board lies on the table itself (spec 2026-09-25 §3.1). The full
  interior is the fully immersive setting, and the judged build offers both.
- **Covers every digital table:** the headset's full-VR mode, the Roblox place and any future one.

## What it doesn't change

- **The board, the rules and the arena (0028, 0037).** The teahouse is spectacle, and spectacle may be
  platform-specific; outcomes may not.
- **0014's art direction** for cards and units: painted faces, pixel sprites. The teahouse is the room
  around them. Its style is warm, lantern-lit and hand-crafted, so it frames 0014's pixel units without
  competing with them.
- **FoV (spec §3.1).** Nothing essential ever sits in a door. The doors live at the edge of view, and the
  board stays within the lower-central 50°.

## Why

JP's vision. It also gives the headset build a place that "couldn't exist anywhere else" (Boldest
Original Concept), and gives cross-play a shared home: a Roblox guest and a headset guest sit in the same
teahouse.
