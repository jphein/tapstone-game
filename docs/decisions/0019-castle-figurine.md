# 0019 — The castle is a figurine with the tag in its base
Date: 2026-09-20 · Brainstorm ruling (Q17)

> **Amended by 0030 (2026-09-23):** the figurine's identity keys the commander in the arena ledger; the record lives there, not on the tag.

Each player's castle is a small figurine: printed base holding an NTAG (424 DNA from the first
real print), a turned wood or resin top, brass ring, a crystal. Setting it on the pad claims your
seat and faction; lifting it is the physical "I'm leaving" in Skirmish and the Realm (Q19). The
figurine carries the player — name, sigil, record — through the signed on-tag history (0017), so a
player brings themselves to a friend's shrines. Cards stay cards. For playtest one, a printed base
with a scrap NTAG215 and any topper will do; the crafted version is photographed with shrine
two-point-oh.
