# 0013 — First set: two factions plus neutrals, 70 designs
Date: 2026-09-20 · Brainstorm ruling (Q7)

**Ember**: fast, cheap units, burn, haste and rush — the beatdown faction. **Tide**: control,
ranged units, bounce and tricks — the tempo faction. Mirrored roles so each has answers to the
other's plan. Plus **10 neutrals** any deck may run. 30 + 30 + 10 = 70 designs, a printable first
run; a deck is one or two factions (0011: 25 cards). Card designs never depend on a fixed opponent
count ("each opponent" scales, "your opponent" does not — Q19). A third faction (Root: growth, big
bodies) is the natural second set.
