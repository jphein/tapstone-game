# 0012 — Three lanes, three cells a side, drawn as vertical columns
Date: 2026-09-20 · Brainstorm ruling (Q6/Q9)

> **Amended by 0028 (2026-09-23):** lanes are drawn on the arena, not mirrored on the shrines.

The battlefield is three lanes; each lane has three cells per side (back, mid, front) and a castle
behind each side. On a shrine it is drawn as three vertical columns, enemy at the top, you at the
bottom, mirrored on the other shrine, with 48 px sprites. Units advance one cell per round unless
blocked; front cells fight the unit facing them or the castle if the facing cells are empty; ranged
units attack from any cell at the nearest enemy in the lane. On the arena's ring (Q18/Q19) lanes run
between adjacent castles; for two players the ring is exactly this board.

Rejected: four cells (36–40 px sprites, one-pixel margins on the shrine) and a free grid (needs
touch every round and fights the one-tap grammar).
