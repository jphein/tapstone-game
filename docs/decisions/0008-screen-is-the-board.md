# 0008 — The screen is the board; cards go to piles
Date: 2026-09-20 · Brainstorm ruling (Q2)

> **Superseded in part by 0028 (2026-09-23):** the board is the arena's screen, not the shrine's. The piles convention stands.

Physical cards are the hand and the deck. Once tapped, a card is set aside beside the shrine:
**face-up for cast, face-down for charge**. The battlefield lives on the screens (shrine mirror or
the arena). No mat is required, nothing can desync, and play stays fast. Units dying, moving or
being bounced are screen events only.

Later experiments that stay open: a printed mat with pressure-sensing cells or a reader per lane
(Q16) can add tactile placement without changing the rules, because the rules never depended on it.
