# 0020 — What the shrine shows when no game is running
Date: 2026-09-20 · Lead's call on Q4 (no ruling needed)

> **Amended by 0032 (2026-09-23):** the idle face shows the commander.

The castle face: your faction's silhouette, the shrine's realm-sigil name, the Familiar if it lives
here, and "set your castle on the stone" as the invitation. Between games in a session: the last
result and the session record. All drawn locally by the framebuffer app (0010); the scry station's
server-streamed faces are not reused (UX study: a no-server table needs local faces).
