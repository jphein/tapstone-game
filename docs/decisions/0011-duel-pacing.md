# 0011 — Duel pacing: 25 cards, 5 in hand, pressure from round 8, hard stop at 12
Date: 2026-09-20 · Brainstorm ruling (Q5)

Decks are 25 cards; opening hand 5 (second player 6, see rules v0); draw one per round. From round
8 every castle takes 2 damage at the start of the round; at round 12 the higher castle wins, ties to
more units on the board, then to the second player. Target match length 6–9 minutes. With
charge-the-shrine (0007) a typical game charges 5–6 cards and casts 12–14; a few are never seen,
which keeps draws mattering.

Every number here is a **keyed-CFG house rule** (`M` key in the protocol draft): deck size, hand
size, pressure round, stop round, castle life, second-player bonus. Tuning is a dashboard change,
applied in the lobby only, never a reflash.
