# Prior art and lineage
Date: 2026-09-19

- **Cards and Castles** (Red Team Games, 2015; ULTIMATE sequel 2025). Cards become animated
  characters on a tactical grid; two-faction decks; sudden death. JP: ~560 hours. The design school
  for Tapstone's lanes, factions and match length. Its name is a live trademark; ours is not a nod.
- **The scry platform** (`~/Projects/scry.realm.watch`, smol `spike-scry`): tap-to-summon station,
  imbue/inscribe rites, HMAC tokens, UID map. Tapstone reuses all of it.
- **realm-cards / artcardsv5**: JP has already built a 63×88 mm card layout pipeline with QR backs and
  an interactive virtual card table. Templates and instincts carry over.
- **NFC CCGs in the field**: to be surveyed (a librarian task): which shipped, which tags they used,
  how they handled cloning, why the phone-app ones stalled.
- **Grimoire** (`~/Projects/magic`): the deck-analysis and rules-of-thumb tooling; a Tapstone deck
  builder can borrow the analysis shape.
