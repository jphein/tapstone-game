Tapstone

Tap the card to the stone

A card game where your cards are real, and the battle comes alive.

See it played

What is it?

Every Tapstone card is a real card you can hold, with a tiny chip hidden inside.

You tap a card on your shrine, a little stone station with a screen and a voice. The shrine reads the card, and it comes alive on the battlefield, on a big screen in the middle of the table.

No phone, and no internet. Just cards, stones and a table.

How a match works

Two players, two castles, three lanes. A match is made to last less than ten minutes.

  1. Claim your seat

    Set your castle on your shrine. Now that seat is yours. Then draw your first cards: each one you draw, you tap on the stone too.

  2. Charge or summon

    Once a turn you can charge: tap a card and lay it face down. It becomes mana, the magic you spend. Or summon: tap a card to send a unit into one of the three lanes.

  3. March and fight

    Your units march forward down their lane. When they meet an enemy, they fight, and both sides hit at the same time.

  4. Knock down their castle

    A castle starts with 20 health. Reach it with your units and spells and knock it down to win.

The Tea House

On a screen or in a headset, every match starts in the Tea House: a warm place full of lanterns, magical tea and comfy seats, where players meet.

When it's time to duel, the red door opens. Step through, and you're on the Dueling Grounds, where the battlefield unrolls.

More doors stand around the Tea House, each one leading to a different realm:

  • the Hearthlands
  • the Deep Tides
  • the Forge Peaks
  • the Wandering Courts
  • the Star Fields
  • the Dreaming
