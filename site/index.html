Tapstonea collectible card game for small shrines · tap a card, the screen becomes the board

Every Tapstone card carries an NFC tag. Tap it on a shrine and the shrine plays it; tap it with a phone and you land on the card's own page. This site is where those pages live.

What is Tapstone? See the promo page.

Set 1: Ember Castle, Tide Castle, Cinder Whelp, Ashen Vanguard, Hearth Warden, Flare, Reef Archer, Tidecaller, Pearl Shieldbearer, Tidal Lash, Undertow, Deep Breath, Mend, Riptide.

In development. Source and design notes: github.com/jphein/tapstone-game.
