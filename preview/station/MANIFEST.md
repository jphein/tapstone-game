# Station preview manifest (decision 0032)

`s*.png` here are 0032's five screens, and `../station-screens.png` is all of them side by side at 2×,
labelled. `motion-*.png` are filmstrips of every motion 0032 names: the *before* screen,
then every frame, each with **its one dirty rectangle outlined in magenta** (the outline is on the
filmstrip only; the device frames under it are exact).

**Stats follow 0034:** every commander is 2/4 at any level; gear is Haste, Taunt or a look.

**Items are real:** set 1's generated item designs from `tapstone-progression` (#65), with their `min_level`; the lobby's stats are progression's `derive_commander`, and the commander holds total XP with its level derived by `level_for_xp`.

**Placeholder, and labelled so:** the commander's name, XP and loadout, the ledger's contents,
and **all the pixel art** (hero, hooded silhouette, gear overlays, chest). **Real:** the castle life, mana charged/spent, hand count,
round and sudden-death round, read from a real **mid-turn** engine state at seed = 21, round 3, after record 37 of 73 (`fx::mid_turn`: seat 0 to act, mana partly spent with some left, a unit in hand it cannot afford). The refusal on `s3-station-refused` is the one the engine returns for casting that unit (`fx::engine_refusal`). **The commander on the station is the engine's** (#49): attack, toughness, keyword, presence and the fall/return house rules are read from the seat, and the fixture game is played with the lobby commander's own claim (`fx::setup`), so the Haste the lobby shows is the Haste in play. The fall and the return (`motion-fall.png`, `motion-return.png`, `s3-station-fallen`) are **real engine transitions** from seed 11 (`fx::FALL_SEED`), where the commander dies in end-of-turn combat as the round turns — so the castle, hand/round and mana wells each settle on a frame of their own. The heal, the struck commander and the waiting (blocked) commander are **real engine records** too, found by `shrine-preview search-states` within seeds 0..2000 × both pickers × three pairings and pinned in `fx::HEAL_AT`, `STRUCK_AT` and `BLOCKED_AT`; a test re-runs the search. The exhausted deck is the one view override left among them: **no seed within that bound reaches it** (12,000 games, 0 replay errors), and a positive control on an 8-card deck shows the search can see one. The full-grid melt (`motion-lootfull.png`) is **unreachable in set 1**: a full grid needs twelve distinct designs and set 1 has six (duplicates melt), so its ledger repeats designs.

| `s5-dark-arena`, `s5-dark-battery` | the dark screen |

## What each motion costs

Costed from the real renders: a frame **pushes** only if it differs from the one before it, and each
push is priced as one window across the per-window range (0.05–4.68 ms), against the 33.3 ms draw
budget. The share is the worst pushed frame at the pessimistic end. `tests/station.rs` recomputes
every row below from the code and fails naming the stale one.

| motion | frames | pushes | max px | worst frame | budget |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| breath (idle) | 20 | 4 | 9,216 | 3.9 – 8.6 ms | 26 % |
| breath | 20 | 4 | 9,216 | 3.9 – 8.6 ms | 26 % |
| struck | 7 | 7 | 12,544 | 5.3 – 10.0 ms | 30 % |
| heal | 5 | 4 | 12,544 | 5.3 – 10.0 ms | 30 % |
| fall | 10 | 10 | 16,464 | 7.0 – 11.6 ms | 35 % |
| return | 9 | 9 | 16,464 | 7.0 – 11.6 ms | 35 % |
| equip | 10 | 9 | 17,640 | 7.5 – 12.1 ms | 36 % |
| xp fill | 6 | 3 | 2,352 | 1.0 – 5.7 ms | 17 % |
| level-up | 9 | 9 | 17,640 | 7.5 – 12.1 ms | 36 % |
| loot | 5 | 5 | 7,680 | 3.3 – 7.9 ms | 24 % |
| loot (full) | 8 | 8 | 7,680 | 3.3 – 7.9 ms | 24 % |
| draw | 4 | 4 | 9,216 | 3.9 – 8.6 ms | 26 % |

The largest frame in every motion stays under half the budget, and no frame dirties more than the
lane column 0025 costed at 40 %. The loot motion's largest frame is **the band**, not the chest: a
state change that touches the figure and the band gives the band its own frame, since pushing both
at once is either two windows or one window over most of the panel.

## Draws are taps (0036, proposed)

`../station-draws.png` shows the draw-tap states on the station, and `d*.png` are those states one
per file. `motion-draw.png`, `motion-drawlast.png` and `motion-mulligan.png` are the filmstrips.
**Every state is one the engine reached** (draw taps are real since #63): the owed count, the
opening hand, the mulligan window, the hand, mana, round and castle, and the "draw first" refusal
(the engine's own `DrawOwed`). The spell draw comes from seed 1 (`fx::SPELL_SEED`), since seed 21
never casts one.

| file | state |
|---|---|
| `d1-opening`, `d1-opening-seat1` | the opening hand owed after the claims: 5 for seat 0, 6 for seat 1 (0035) |
| `d2-opening-mid` | 3 drawn, 2 owed |
| `d3-mulligan-offer` | the mulligan window, with the HAND half of its well as the touch target |
| `d3b-mulligan-prompt` | a castle tapped in the window: the 3 s prompt (the lead's ruling), with nothing sent yet |
| `d4-mulliganed` | after a mulligan, a fresh opening hand owed |
| `d5-turn-start`, `d6-refused` | the turn-start draw, and another tap refused while it is owed |
| `d7-spell-draw` | a spell that draws two |
| `d8-deck-empty` | an exhausted deck, which owes nothing (**view override**: no seed within 0..2000 × both pickers × three pairings reaches one; `search-states`) |
